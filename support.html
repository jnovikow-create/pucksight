Holding the phone

About 40 cm from your eyes. Me includes a distance stepper when you train closer or farther. Landscape drills want the phone sideways, with thumbs on the two halves when the drill asks for that.

Side Light

Set the phone in landscape, glass toward you, hands out to each side. Sides lights a small box on the far left or the far right. Corners lights one of the four corners. Move that hand toward the box, up on a top corner and down on a bottom corner. Colour changes each cue, and the pace climbs through the set. The front camera judges the motion. The frames remain on the handset and are not kept. Show camera is optional and lets you look at stance, glove, and blocker while you react. That view is not stored. If camera access was declined, the drill points you to Settings. Use a lit room.

Flashing

A few drills use a short flash. Switch on Reduce flashing in Me when you or someone in your family has seizures or epilepsy, and speak with a physician before you train. The drills also sit under a hard flash-rate cap.

What a score means

The Vision Score is your own index of crease-drill results on that handset. An eye exam is a different visit. A concussion screen is a different visit. Save percentage is a separate number.

Printed sheets

Share a chart from the off-ice exercise and print it. The far Hart chart is the one to tape at eye level for Near-Far Focus. The phone is the near chart. Ball work needs a clear space. Stop if anything doubles or a headache starts, and see an optometrist or ophthalmologist if vision changes.

Reminder

The daily reminder stays off until you enable it and pick a time in Me. PuckSight stays silent from 9:30 p.m. to 7:00 a.m.

Premium

Included play covers the core drills, week 1 of Goalie Foundations, the pre-game warm-up, and 30 days of history. Premium is monthly, yearly with a 7-day trial, or lifetime. It opens Low-Light, higher levels, the longer history, CSV export, and the Goalie Crease theme. Restore purchases is in Me. If those products are still absent from sale, the paywall says so and the included drills still run.

Clear the log

Me, then Delete all data, removes the profile, sessions, and logs from the handset.
