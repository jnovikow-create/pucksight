Privacy Policy

Effective October 21, 2026 · Developer: Justin Novikow · Bundle ID com.jnovikow.PuckSight

Short version: Your drills, logs, and profile stay on your iPhone. There is no account. This version does not show ads and does not send your scores to Justin Novikow.

1. Stored on your iPhone

The app saves this on the device:

  • Your name, position, catch hand, level, and training settings
  • Drill sessions, the Vision Score history, streaks, and badges
  • Off-ice logs and the plan you are on
  • Whether a parent confirmed an under-13 profile

Justin Novikow does not receive a copy. Deleting the app, or using Delete all data in Me, removes this save from the phone. A device backup can still hold an older copy.

2. What the app does not collect

  • No account and no sign-in
  • No location
  • No advertising identifier in this version
  • No sale of your training log
  • No tracking across other companies' apps

If a later version adds ads, this page will say so before that build ships. Ads will not run during a drill.

3. Camera

Side Light uses the front camera to see which hand moved when a side of the screen lights up. The frames are processed on the iPhone. They are not saved, not added to Photos, and not sent to Justin Novikow or anyone else. The other drills do not use the camera. You can say no. Side Light then tells you how to allow the camera in iOS Settings.

4. Purchases

Premium is sold by Apple. Apple processes the payment. PuckSight reads your entitlement on the device so locked drills can open. Justin Novikow does not receive your card number.

5. Notifications

If you turn on the daily reminder, iOS schedules it at the time you pick. The app stays quiet overnight. You can turn the reminder off in the app or in iOS Settings.

6. Children

An under-13 path asks a parent to confirm. That choice is stored on the phone. PuckSight is not in the App Store Kids category. Brock string and similar exercises stay off for that profile unless a parent turns them on.

7. Contact

Questions: jnovikow@gmail.com

PuckSight is a sports-training app, not a medical device.
