Privacy

Updated October 22, 2026 · Justin Novikow · com.jnovikow.PuckSight

PuckSight is a goalie vision station. Drill results, the Vision Score, and off-ice notes sit in the app. That log is never delivered to the developer. You do not create credentials for PuckSight.

What the handset keeps

  • Name, position, catch hand, level, and training settings
  • Drill sessions, Vision Score history, streaks, and badges
  • Off-ice logs and which plan is active
  • A parent confirmation when the player is under 13

Delete all data, under Me, clears that save. Removing the app clears it too. An iPhone backup may still contain an older copy.

Side Light and the camera

Side Light uses the front camera to judge which hand moved when a box lights. The handset does the processing. Frames are not stored, not placed in Photos, and not sent to Justin Novikow or anyone else. Every other drill leaves the camera off. If you decline access, Side Light tells you where to allow it in iOS Settings.

Show camera, if you switch it on, puts that same live picture behind the boxes so you can study stance, glove, and blocker. The picture is not a recording.

Premium

Apple sells PuckSight Premium and handles the payment. The app reads the entitlement on the handset so a locked drill can open. Your card number stays with Apple.

Daily reminder

The reminder stays off until you pick a time. Quiet hours run from 9:30 p.m. to 7:00 a.m. Stop it under Me.

Younger players

The under-13 path asks a parent to confirm, and that choice sits with the log. PuckSight is not offered in the Kids category. Brock string stays off on that profile unless a parent enables it.

Banners

Version 1.0 ships without an ad banner. If a future build adds one, this page will change first, and a drill will not be cut off by it.
