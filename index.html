PuckSight

Vision training for hockey. Track the puck, read the release, and warm up the skills goalies use. No extra hardware.

See it sooner.

What it does

On-ice reads, on the phone

Puck tracking, flash reads, glove or blocker, fakes, post-to-post, and a quiet-eye hold. Read the Release uses an animated shooter.

A score that stays yours

A personal Vision Score from your drill results. It is a training index on this phone, not a clinical test and not a save prediction.

Off the ice too

Nineteen off-ice exercises with steps, a timer, a log, and charts you can print. Use a clear space.

On the phone

Help Support Distance, flashing, reminders, and Premium. Privacy Privacy policy Sessions stay on the iPhone. There is no account.
